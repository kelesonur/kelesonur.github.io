Features in the environment

Natural classes can also describe the environment. English past tense -ed is voiceless after a voiceless sound: [wɔkt] ‘walked’, but [pleɪd] ‘played’.

/d/ → −voice / −voice

Choose the allophone with the wider distribution as the underlying form. Then the rule only needs to describe the special case.

Practice: translate between prose and rules

Prose to rule[k] becomes [x] between vowels.
/k/ → [x] / V V
Prose to rule[z] becomes [s] at the end of a word.
/z/ → [s] / #
Prose to rule[a] is nasalized before a nasal consonant.
/a/ → [ã] / +nasal
Rule to prose/t/ → [ɾ] / V __ V
/t/ becomes a flap [ɾ] between vowels, as in English [foɾəɡɹæf] ‘photograph’.
Rule to prose/p/ → [pʰ] / # __
/p/ is aspirated at the start of a word, as in English [pʰat] ‘pot’ but [spat] ‘spot’.
Rule to prose/n/ → [ŋ] / __ [+velar]
/n/ becomes [ŋ] before a velar consonant, as in [ɪŋkəɹɛkt] ‘incorrect’.
