Phonle

[fonəl]

Guess the hidden English word, written in IPA. Tap a colored tile after a guess to see how it compares.
 

How to play

Find the hidden English word in 6 guesses. Each tile is one sound, not one letter. So [ʃɪp] ship has 3 sounds, and [tʃ], [dʒ] and the diphthongs [aɪ] [aʊ] [ɔɪ] each take one tile.

s
Right sound in the right spot.
ɪ
The sound is in the word, but in another spot.
k
The sound is not in the word.

Feature dots. Under each tile that is not green, dots compare your sound with the hidden sound in that same spot. A filled dot means the feature matches.

  • Consonants: V voicing, P place, M manner.
  • Vowels: H height, B backness, R rounding, T tense or lax.
  • A small C or V in the corner means the hidden sound in that spot is a consonant or a vowel instead.

We use the symbols from class: [e] as in bait, [o] as in boat, [a] as in pot, [ɔ] as in bought, [r] for English r.

Play with the class: everyone picks the same length and puzzle number, then presses Play this puzzle.
