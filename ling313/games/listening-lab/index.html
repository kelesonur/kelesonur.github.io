Does the synthetic voice follow Turkish phonology?

In Part A, place the Turkish vowels on an empty vowel chart and compare them with IPA reference vowels. In Part B, a Turkish text-to-speech voice reads 18 words from this week's topics: write down what you hear and compare it with what the rules from class predict. In Part C, measure vowel length on a waveform and spectrogram, for the voice and for your own pronunciation.

Turkish words: ElevenLabs, voice “Mehmet Akif”, model Multilingual v2, read as a list.
